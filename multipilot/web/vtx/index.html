Find your VTX channel and check its power

Not sure which channel a quad's video transmitter is on? Power the quad up near a C5MK receiver and this page scans the 5.8 GHz bands, names the channel, shows how far the VTX sits from the channel's centre frequency, and compares its signal with a reference you saved earlier.

  • Scans Raceband, Fatshark and Boscam A, B and E channels from 5180 to 5917 MHz
  • Flags a transmitter that's off its channel's centre frequency
  • Compares power against a saved reference, such as a known 25 mW VTX
  • Runs in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android, with nothing to install

Questions

Is this a calibrated power meter?

No. The comparison is relative: it tells you whether a VTX is stronger or weaker than your reference at the same distance, not its output in milliwatts.

What hardware do I need?

An ESP32-C5 board with a 5 GHz antenna running the free C5MK firmware, which you can flash from the C5 flasher, and a USB data cable.

Can the same receiver time laps?

Yes. Use the browser lap timer for 1–4 pilots, or plug it into RotorHazard for up to eight.
