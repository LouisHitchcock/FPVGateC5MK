Power check

How it works: put a quad you know is 25 mW at a marked spot (same distance, same way round), and save it as the reference. Then put each quad on the same spot. 200 mW reads about 9 dB higher, 600 mW about 14 dB. This compares against your reference; it isn't a calibrated power meter, and walls, hands and antenna angle all move the reading a dB or two.
